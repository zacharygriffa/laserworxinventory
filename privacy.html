LaserWorx Inventory — Privacy Policy

Effective: 9/29/2026

LaserWorx Inventory ("the App") is an internal tool used by LaserWorx employees to look up and adjust inventory quantities in the company's own QuickBooks Online account. It is not offered to the public.

Information the App accesses

  • QuickBooks Online data: product/item records (name, SKU, quantity on hand, reorder point) in the connected LaserWorx QuickBooks company.
  • Authentication tokens: OAuth tokens issued by Intuit so the App can access QuickBooks on the user's behalf.
  • Camera: used only on the device to read barcodes. Images are processed on the device and are not stored or transmitted.
  • Usage history: a log of inventory removals and count adjustments (item, quantity, date), stored on the device to estimate minimum stock levels.

How information is used

Data is used solely to display inventory, record stock removals and cycle counts, and calculate suggested reorder points for LaserWorx. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the device. Inventory data is sent only between the device and Intuit's QuickBooks Online servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The App has no separate server and does not collect data anywhere else.

Disconnecting

Logging out of the App deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from QuickBooks Online under Settings → Apps / Connected apps.
